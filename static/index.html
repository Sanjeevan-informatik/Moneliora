PLAIN ENGLISH

What is paper trading?

It's a practice version of investing. You choose shares with fictional prices and spend imaginary euros. Your real bank account is never involved.

It's useful for learning how orders and portfolios work—but it cannot predict actual investment performance.

LEARNING CENTRE

Learn the basics

Short, practical explanations—no finance jargon required.

01

Shares

A share represents a small ownership interest in a company. Its price can rise or fall, and you could lose money.

02

ETFs

An exchange-traded fund can hold many assets. Diversification can reduce company-specific risk, but does not eliminate losses.

03

Fees & spreads

Costs can include an order fee and the spread between buying and selling prices. They reduce your investment return.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Good things to know

Is Moneliora a stockbroker?

No. This learning app cannot place orders or hold real investments.

Are these today's stock prices?

No. The displayed figures are fixed fictional examples for educational calculations.

Do I need to register?

No. Your practice holdings and watchlist are kept in your browser, not a user account. Clearing site data may erase them.

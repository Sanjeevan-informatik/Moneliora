THE OFFER TO VALIDATE FIRST

Free utility → paid guidance → repeatable delivery.

Keep the calculators free to demonstrate value. Test a bounded educational session with a concrete worksheet. Consider recurring products only after observing repeat demand and enough margin to fund support.

The proposed €49 and €99 prices are hypotheses. Compare the price customers accept against the full cost of delivery; your own time is a cost.

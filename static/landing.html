CLEAR EXPECTATIONS

A few things
worth knowing.

Is Moneliora a financial adviser or broker?

No. It is an educational tool. It does not recommend securities, assess suitability, execute investments or manage your money.

Are the forecasts guaranteed?

The tools show scenarios based on your assumptions. They do not forecast markets or guarantee outcomes. Real returns, costs and inflation vary.

Where do my figures go?

The free planning tools calculate in your browser. Optional saved plans are unencrypted on your device. A pilot interest request, if enabled, sends only the information in that form to the operator.

Can I buy a session today?

There is no checkout for these pilot sessions. If requests are open, you may express interest. A session is only confirmed after a separate agreement with the operator.
